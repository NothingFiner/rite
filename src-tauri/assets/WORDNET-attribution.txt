What was changed: WordNet's synsets were flattened to one row per sense
(lemma, part of speech, sense number, gloss, synonyms, antonyms), multi-word
lemmas were dropped, and the result was compressed with brotli. The generator
is scripts/build-thesaurus.py; the licence of the derived asset is unchanged.
